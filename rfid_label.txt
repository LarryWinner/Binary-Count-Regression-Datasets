Dataset: rfid_label.csv

Source: J. Singh, E. Olsen, K. Vorst, K. Tripp (2009). "RFID Tag
Readability Issues with Palletized Loads of Consumer Goods,"
Packaging Technology and Science, Vol. 22, pp. 431-441.

Description: Experiment was conducted to examine Success/Failure
of reading of Radio Frequency Identification on pallets of consumer goods.
Response: Success/Failure of Reading RFID labels
Predictors: Speed (1.62, 8.05, 16.09 km/hr),
Pallet Pattern (1 = Column, 2 = Interlocking, 3 = Pinwheel)
Product (1 = Bottled Water, 2 = Carbonated Beverage Can)

Each pallet went through 5 passes, but Pass was not a factor in model.

Variable names:
product
pattern
speed
passNum
numRead
numCase    (number of cases on pallet)
