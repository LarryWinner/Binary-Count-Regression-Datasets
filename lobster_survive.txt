Dataset: lobster_survive.dat

Source: E.B. Wilkinson, J.H. Grabowski, G.D. Sherwood, P.O. Yund (2015).
"Influence of Predator Identity on the Strength of Predator Avoidance
Response in Lobsters," Journal of Experimental Biology and Ecology,
Vol. 465, pp. 107-112.

Description: Lobster survival during tethering experiments by carpace length.
Data at individual lobster level (grouped in 3 mm length increments).

Variables/Columns
Carpace Length in mm   7-8
Survive      16  /* 0=No, 1=Yes  */
