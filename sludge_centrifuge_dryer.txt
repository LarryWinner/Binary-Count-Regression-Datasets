Dataset: sludge_centrifuge_dryer.csv

Source: B. Peeters, R. Dewil, I.Y. Smets (2012). "Improved Process Control
of an Industrial Sludge Centrifuge-Dryer Installation Through Binary
Logistic Regression Modeling of the Fouling Issues," Journal of Process
Control, Vol. 22, pp. 1387-1396.

Description: Results of an experiment with 3 factors on presence/absence
of fouling by a sudge centrifuge dryer.
Factors:
Sludge Volume Index   /* 1 = < 60 mL/g,  2= > 60 */
Flotation sludge to Feed  /* 1= No addition, 2=Addition, 3=polyaluminumchloride*/
Clay Dosing   /* 1=Low, 2=Medium, 3=High  */

Variables
SVI
sludgefd
claydose
totcase     /* Number of observations */
numfoul    /* number fouling */
