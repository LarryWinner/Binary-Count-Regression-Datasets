Dataset:  brit_rail_acc.dat

Source: Andrew W. Evans (2007). "Rail Safety and rail Privatization in Britain,"
Accident Analysis and Prevention, Vol. 39, pp. 510-523.

Description:
Numbers of fata rail accidents and millions of main line train km annually
in Britain for 1946-2003. Rail was privatised in 1994. Author fits Poisson 
regression for 1967-2003 overall fatalites per train mile as primary analysis.

Variables/Columns
Year    5-8
Main line train (millions of km) 10-16
collision, derailment and overrun accidents   22-24
collision, derailment and overrun fatalities  28-32
Train/road vehicle collision accidents  36-40 
Train/road vehicle collision fatalities  44-48
Movements and non-movement accidents   52-56 
Movements and non-movement accidents fatalities    60-64
