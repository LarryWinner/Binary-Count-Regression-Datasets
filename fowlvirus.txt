Dataset: fowlvirus.dat

Source: D.B.W. Reid, J.F. Crawley, and A.J. Rhodes (1949). "A Study of Fowl
Pox Virus Titration on the Chorio-Allantois by Pock Counting Technique,"
Journal of Immunology, Vol.63 pp. 165-171

Description: Pock Counts per membrane for 50 embryos exposed to
one of 4 virus dilutions (multiples of 10^(-3.86).

Variables/Columns
Dilution factor   4-8
Number of pocks observed   15-16
