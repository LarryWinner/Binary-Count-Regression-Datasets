Dataset: apprentice.dat

Source: A. Lovett and R. Flowerdew (1989). "Analysis of Count Data Using
Poisson Regression," Professional Geographer, 41, pp. 190-198.

Description: Apprentice migration between 1775 and 1799 to Edinburgh from
33 regions in Scotland. Can be used to fit a Poisson Regression
relating number of apprentices migrating from region to: Distance,
population, degree of urbanization, and direction from Edinburgh.

Variables/Columns:
County Name    1-20
Distance    21-23
Apprentices    28-31
Population (1000s)   36-39
Degree of Urbanization   43-47
Direction from Edinburgh   55  /* 1=North, 2=West, 3=South  */
