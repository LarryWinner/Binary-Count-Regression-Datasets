Dataset:  afghan_terror.dat

Source:J.A. Piazza (2012). "The Opium Trade and Patterns of Terrorism
in the Provinces of Afghanistan: An Empirical Analysis," Terrorism and
Political Violence, Vol. 24, pp. 213-234

Description: Variables relating to province's terrorism (incidennce and casualties) 
in Afghanistan.
Variables include: opium production, population, area, mountainous, nutrition, literacy,
drinking water, roads, infant mortality, Pashtun majority, and Foreign troops.
Author fit negative binomial regression, based on annual data (1994-2008).
This data is aggregated over years (n=33).

Variables/Columns
Province    1-12
Total Terrorism incidents   17-20
Total Terrorism casualties  22-28
Average Opium Cultivation (hectares)  30-36
Population (1000s)       38-44
Area (1000s km^2)        46-52
Mountainous (%)     54-60
Literacy Rate (%?)      64-68
Access to Drinking water (%)  74-76
Below minimum calories (%)   82-84
All-season Roads  (%)   90-92
Under 5 Mortality (per 1000)  96-100
Pashtun Majority  (1=Yes,0=No)  108
Average Foreign Troops (per year) 110-116
