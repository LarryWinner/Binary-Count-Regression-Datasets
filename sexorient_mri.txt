Dataset: sexorient_mri.csv

Source: J. Ponsetti, O. Granert, O. Jansen, S. Wolff, H. Mehdorn, H. Bosinski, H. Siebner (2009).
"Assessment of Sexual Orientation Using the Hemodynamic Brain Response to Visual Sexual Stimuli,"
Journal of Sexual Medicine, Vol. 6,pp. 1628-1634.

Description: Classification of 26 individuals (14 homosexual, 12 heterosexual) to sexual
orientation based on MRI expression values. Data approximated from Figure 2, page 1632.
Authors classified based on Fisher's Linear Discriminant Analysis and K-nearest neighbors (K=7).
Classification was focused on heterosexual in terms of sensitivity and specificity.

Variable Names: 
MRIEV     (MRI Expresson value x 10^5)
Group   (1=Homosexual, 2=Heterosexual)
