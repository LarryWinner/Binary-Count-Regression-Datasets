Dataset: criminal_clothing.csv

Source: S.A. Wetmore, J.S. Neuschatz, S.D. Gronlund, K.N. Key, C.A. Goodsell
(2015). "Do Clothes Make the Criminal? Thhe Influence of Clothing Match on
Identification Accuracy in Showups," Journal of Applied Research in Memory
and Cognition, Vol. 4, pp. 36-42.

Description: Experiment on positive identification of a suspect
varying 3 factors: 
Perpetrator Present/Absent, 
Shirt worn matching/not matching description
Hat worn matching/not matching description
Note: Number reported as Positive ID in PerpetratorAbsent/ShirtHatMismatch
condition should be 43 (not 119) in Table 1 (confirmed by calculations 
reported in paper and data in Appendix A).

Variable Names
perpPrsnt   (1=Present,0=Absent)
shirtMatch   (1=Yes,0=No)
hatMatch  (1=Yes, 0=No)
posID     (1=Yes, 0=No)
